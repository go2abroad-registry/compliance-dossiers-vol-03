Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is the federal tax administration; registration and filings are processed through Business Registration Online (BRO) and My Business Account portal. The British Columbia Ministry of Finance administers provincial sales tax (PST), employer health tax, and BC corporate registration via Corporate Online and eTaxBC.

E-government filing portal

A foreign SPAC must obtain a Canadian federal Business Number (BN) through CRA Business Registration Online using Form RC1 or RC1A. A GST/HST program account should be opened when taxable supplies exceed C$30,000 in any single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters. Voluntary GST/HST registration is advisable before closing acquisition transactions. The foreign corporation must submit its certified constitutional documents, foreign registration details, a BC registered office address, and a resident BC agent for service. Federal incorporation through Corporations Canada produces a number integrated with CRA accounts; provincially incorporated BC corporations receive a BC incorporation number linked to the federal BN. Separate CRA program accounts for payroll and indirect tax remittances must be created as activities commence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Canada does not provide U.S.-style check-the-box or pass-through elections for a business corporation. A Canadian-resident SPAC is taxed at entity level on worldwide income. Available elections include the section 216 election for non-residents to compute Canadian-source net income on a net basis; subsection 217 election for non-resident employees; associate election under section 156 of the Excise Tax Act to treat closely related GST/HST registrants as one registrant; and the section 21 election to capitalize interest and foreign exchange costs on acquisition debt. Hybrid mismatch rules in the Income Tax Act are mandatory and documentation-driven rather than elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

C$30,000 of worldwide taxable supplies, other than exempt supplies, over four consecutive calendar quarters or in one calendar quarter; separate BC PST registration is required for vendors making taxable sales in BC, with applicable small-seller exemptions only for limited out-of-province sellers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from Canada by the supplier qualify for zero-rating under section 1 of Schedule VI to the Excise Tax Act if the supplier possesses documentary evidence of export and delivery occurs outside Canada. Services performed directly to non-residents and related to exported goods, or performed wholly outside Canada, may also be zero-rated where the conditions of Schedule VI are met. For BC PST, deliveries of tangible goods shipped directly out of BC to foreign destinations are generally exempt from PST with fully documented proof of export. SPAC financial and trust-related services normally fall within exempt financial services rather than zero-rated supplies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Canada Border Services Agency Form A6 or B13A) filed through the Canadian Export Reporting System
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or cargo receipt evidencing physical delivery to the non-resident consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/wire/FX settlement confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds from the foreign purchaser

Canada does not currently mandate country-wide real-time fiscal clearance, digital tax stamping, or continuous transaction control (CTC) e-invoicing. CRA accepts electronic invoices meeting standard readability and integrity requirements. GST/HST registrants above threshold must file and remit electronically using GST/HST NETFILE, XML, or CRA-supported API channels. BC PST returns are submitted electronically through eTaxBC. No pre-clearance or government-controlled e-invoicing repository exists.

CRA may reassess beyond the normal four-year period where misrepresentation or fraud is established. Claiming zero-rating without adequate export evidence results in denial and clawback of input tax credits and refunds, gross-negligence penalties of 20% of the claimed amounts, false-statement penalties under section 280.1 of the Excise Tax Act, and interest accrual. Criminal prosecution for wilful tax evasion can impose fines up to 200% of the tax evaded and imprisonment up to five years. BC PST audits similarly may reassess, deny exemption claims, and apply prescribed interest and civil penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies to both capital and current account items for residents and non-residents. No Bank of Canada or Department of Finance authorization is required for inbound SPAC IPO subscription remittances, escrow deposits, redemption payments, or business-combination disbursements. Profit and capital repatriation is unfettered, subject only to Part XIII non-resident withholding tax (statutory 25%, typically reduced under tax treaty, on dividends and certain interest to non-residents) and to information reporting obligations to the Canada Revenue Agency ('CRA') (e.g., Form T1135 for specified foreign property and Form T1134 for foreign affiliates, where triggered). The SPAC may hold escrow proceeds in USD, CAD, or any freely convertible currency without restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Canada imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation deadline for export proceeds or any cross-border receipts, including IPO and escrow inflow. Funds may remain offshore or onshore indefinitely in any currency. The only compliance hooks are transparency requirements: FINTRAC mandatory reporting of electronic funds transfers of CAD 10,000 or more, CRA information returns for foreign property/affiliates, and FATCA/CRS account reporting by the financial institution. No approval or declaration to the Bank of Canada is required at any time.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada) as monetary authority under the Bank of Canada Act; Canada maintains a free-floating exchange-rate regime with no dedicated foreign-exchange control agency. OSFI (Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions) supervises all federally chartered banks and trust companies; FINTRAC administers cross-border transaction monitoring and reporting under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA) and associated regulations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for capital escrow and trust custody; MEDIUM for ancillary operational payments. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, etc.) are not authorized deposit-taking institutions in Canada and cannot act as custodian or escrow holder for SPAC IPO proceeds under the Trust and Loan Companies Act (Canada) or BC Financial Institutions Act. They may provide limited transactional services (FX conversion, payroll and vendor payments) only if registered or exempt under the Retail Payment Activities Act (RPAA) transitional regime, and any use must be supplemental to, not a replacement for, an OSFI-regulated bank or trust company clearing rail.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Complex offshore beneficial-ownership structure (Cayman/BVI holding layers, nominee shareholders, or discretionary trusts above the SPAC)

Root cause: Complex offshore beneficial-ownership structure (Cayman/BVI holding layers, nominee shareholders, or discretionary trusts above the SPAC)

Defense evidence: Certified ownership chain from ultimate individual beneficial owners through each intermediary; CRS/FATCA self-certifications (Canada Form RC518); certificates of good standing for each entity; legal opinion from Canadian and offshore counsel confirming economic substance, absence of bearer shares, and legitimacy of trust/nominee arrangements; audited financial statements or opening balance-sheet pack; sponsor and promoter biographies with independent professional references.
Bulk inbound subscription wires from multiple unrelated subscribers lacking commercial or trade underlying documentation

Root cause: Bulk inbound subscription wires from multiple unrelated subscribers lacking commercial or trade underlying documentation

Defense evidence: Signed subscription agreements per subscriber; final prospectus or offering memorandum receipted by the British Columbia Securities Commission (BCSC) or Canadian Securities Administrators; escrow agreement among the SPAC, bank, and qualified custodian/trustee; board resolutions authorizing share issuance and escrow deposit instructions; SWIFT MT103 payment trail with subscriber identification for all material positions; transfer-agent allocation schedule.
Escrow release and redemption payments to non-resident shareholders following business-combination announcement or shareholder vote

Root cause: Escrow release and redemption payments to non-resident shareholders following business-combination announcement or shareholder vote

Defense evidence: Executed business-combination agreement; shareholder redemption notices and redemption register; board minutes approving continuation, amalgamation, or dissolution; Part XIII withholding calculation and CRA remittance advice (proprietary or NR4 forms); fund-flow statement mapping escrow release to lawful consideration; target-company valuation and fairness opinion; shareholder residence and nexus-status declarations.
Politically exposed persons (PEPs) or sanctioned-jurisdiction nationals among sponsors, directors, or 10%+ shareholders

Root cause: Politically exposed persons (PEPs) or sanctioned-jurisdiction nationals among sponsors, directors, or 10%+ shareholders

Defense evidence: Bank-prescribed PEP declarations from each relevant individual; independent enhanced due diligence report from an internationally recognized screening provider; negative sanction-list findings across OSFI, OFAC, UN and EU lists; independent director oversight framework with documented enhanced monitoring procedures; source-of-wealth and source-of-funds reports supported by bank statements, tax returns, and asset-ownership evidence.
Inconsistent transactional patterns between operating account and escrow account flagged during periodic transaction monitoring

Root cause: Inconsistent transactional patterns between operating account and escrow account flagged during periodic transaction monitoring

Defense evidence: Adopted treasury policy with mandatory segregation schedule; monthly reconciliation between escrow trust balance, operating ledger, CRA filings, and audited financial statements; expense-support package comprising vendor agreements, itemized invoices, wire-transfer forms with descriptive purpose codes; independent auditor's management letter confirming control environment and absence of commingling.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

IPO subscription proceeds are routed via SWIFT MT103 through the Canadian bank's correspondent network into the dedicated SPAC escrow account held with an OSFI-regulated bank or trust company. Field 70 (Remittance Information) must carry a descriptive narrative such as 'SPAC [ticker] IPO subscription – escrow per prospectus dated [date]'; field 50K ordering party must match the subscriber in the final allocation schedule. The bank places funds in 'pending verification' status until the transfer agent's share-allocation certificate is delivered. USD subscriptions clear via bank-internal US dollar clearing facilities (e.g., Fedwire through a US affiliate or correspondent); CAD conversions, where elected, are executed at bank-published spot with no governmental approval. Over-subscription refunds are returned via MT103 RE referencing the original message to prevent layering alerts.
