Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) / Agence du revenu du Canada - federal tax registration through Business Registration Online (BRO) and filing via My Business Account at canada.ca; British Columbia provincial indirect taxation is administered by the BC Ministry of Finance, Consumer Taxation Branch (eTaxBC portal for PST and carbon tax); Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) administers customs export declarations and the Excise Act, 2001 fuel duty regime.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Incorporate the BC Unlimited Liability Corporation in the Province of British Columbia via the BC Corporate Registry (Corporate Online), obtaining the incorporation number and 10-digit BC Company Identifier. Step 2 - Apply to CRA for the federal 9-digit Business Number (BN) with the required program accounts: GST/HST (RT), Import/Export (RM), and Payroll (RP) where Canadian employees are engaged; for non-resident applicants CRA Form RC3663 must be submitted, supported by an Individual Tax Number (ITN) obtained under the non-resident income tax registration process. Step 3 - Obtain an Excise Duty licence under the Excise Act, 2001 when the ULC physically sells, stores, or distributes marine fuel oils in Canada; a pure commission-based wholesale broker may not require the excise licence but must verify counter-party licences. Step 4 - Register as a BC PST vendor with the BC Ministry of Finance (eTaxBC) before making taxable sales in British Columbia; export sales, resale sales with PST resale certificates, and most cross-border marine fuel supplies are exempt. Step 5 - Complete CRA and BC registrations electronically with immediate effect for GST/HST and BC PST; maintain the BN in all invoicing and keep the registration certificates available for counter-party due diligence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - a BC ULC is a Canadian-resident corporation under the Income Tax Act (Canada) and files T2 corporate income tax returns on worldwide income with foreign tax credit relief; Pass_Through_Eligible solely for US federal tax purposes because the unlimited liability attribute allows US shareholders to file Form 8832 and elect disregarded entity or partnership classification; Canadian domestic flow-through treatment is not available to ULC shareholders.

Available election forms

US federal check-the-box election (Form 8832) by non-resident shareholders of the ULC; Canadian functional-currency election under subsection 261(2) ITA for computation of tax in US dollars; hybrid mismatch arrangement filing and designation regime under ITA sections 18.2 to 18.8 (Part 19) covering structured hybrid arrangements; section 216 ITA election for non-residents deriving Canadian rental or royalty income; GST/HST voluntary registration election for non-residents and small-supplier threshold waiver or revocation election; BC PST resale certificate election and inventory transitional election for goods brought into BC.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 - the GST/HST small-supplier threshold measured on total taxable supplies (including zero-rated supplies but excluding financial services and capital real property sales) over four consecutive calendar quarters; the threshold generally applies to non-residents making supplies in Canada; no PST registration threshold exists in BC - every vendor must hold a valid PST vendor number before making taxable sales; an excise fuel licence is triggered by first receipt or delivery of dutiable fuel in Canada.

Canada does not operate a continuous transaction control (CTC) regime or require real-time fiscal clearance, government API stamping, or digital tax stamps for fuel oil wholesale brokerage; CRA fully accepts EDI, XML, and PEPPOL-based Pan-Canadian B2B e-invoicing on a voluntary basis; GST/HST returns must be filed electronically through GST/HST NETFILE, and books and records must be retained for six years in an accessible electronic format; exporters must retain an electronic export documentation package in accordance with ETA section 169 and CRA GST/HST Memorandum 7-9; BC carbon-tax and PST returns are filed through the eTaxBC portal with volumetric reporting obligations for fuel suppliers.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada maintains a free current account convertibility regime under IMF Article VIII. Capital account transactions are liberalized with limited prior approval requirements from the Bank of Canada or OSFI for investments above thresholds. Routine profit remittances, dividends, and shareholder distributions to foreign owners of ULCs are permitted without restriction, subject only to tax filing and FINTRAC AML compliance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds required under Canadian law. Exporters must file export declarations with the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) within statutory timelines and submit suspicious transaction reports to FINTRAC. Proceeds may be retained in CAD or converted at prevailing market rates.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada
