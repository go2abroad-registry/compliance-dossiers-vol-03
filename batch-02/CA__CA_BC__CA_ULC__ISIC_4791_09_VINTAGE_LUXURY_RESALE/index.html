Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Partially Permissible with Compliance Conditions: BCA s 25 requires a registered office located in BC at a physical address where service of process may be delivered; the registered records office under s 29 must be a physical location in BC open during business hours and capable of producing statutory records within required timeframes. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Under the Canada-U.S. Tax Convention Article IV(7), a Canadian ULC may elect to be treated as a corporation for U.S. federal tax purposes; absent such election, the ULC may be treated as a fiscally transparent/pass-through entity for U.S. purposes. For Canadian income tax, the ULC remains an entity-level taxpayer. GST/HST rules allow related-corporation elections under Section 156 of the Excise Tax Act to avoid tax on certain intercompany supplies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 in taxable supplies made in Canada during any preceding four consecutive calendar quarters; non-resident online platform operators may be required to register without the small-supplier threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from Canada by a GST/HST registrant are zero-rated when title passes to a non-resident buyer and the goods are exported in a continuous unbroken flow of transport, or delivered outside Canada by the supplier or the non-resident purchaser. Certain offshore services performed for a non-resident are also zero-rated. For vintage luxury resale e-commerce, each sale must be supported by documentary proof of export to the non-resident customer.

  • Canadian Customs Export Declaration (CBSA GCA or approved commercial system)
  • Bill of Lading or waybill evidencing foreign destination
  • Commercial invoice, payment records, and inward remittance certificate or foreign bank statement when remittance scrutiny applies

Canada imposes no real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping. GST/HST returns and invoices are transmitted electronically using GST/HST NETFILE. E-invoicing is voluntary and interoperable through the Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 framework, but not yet statutory.

CRA may disallow the zero-rating, reassess uncollected GST/HST at the point of sale, and impose interest and penalties. Knowingly or fraudulently claiming zero-rating without required export proof triggers civil penalties up to 20% of the net tax amount, recovery for a 4-year reassessment window (extended indefinitely for misrepresentation), and potential criminal prosecution for tax evasion.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Canadian dollar is fully convertible under a free-floating exchange-rate regime. Canada imposes no exchange-control restrictions on either current-account or capital-account transactions. Inbound equity and debt capital may be freely subscribed or lent into a British Columbia ULC; outbound profit remittance by dividend is unrestricted under the British Columbia Business Corporations Act (SBC 2002, c. 57), subject only to board solvency tests and non-resident Part XIII withholding tax (statutory 25%, treaty-reducible). For ISIC 4791 online luxury resale, the only compliance duties are reporting-based: FINTRAC EFT reports for wire transfers at or above C$10,000, large cash transaction reports at or above C$10,000, MSB registration for payment processors, and functional-currency tax reporting under Income Tax Act section 261 if the ULC books are kept in a foreign currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender rule applies in Canada. Exporters are not subject to any timeline for repatriation, any mandatory conversion ratio, or any export-proceeds certificate. The BC ULC may retain USD, EUR, or other foreign-currency proceeds indefinitely in a foreign-currency operating account maintained with a Canadian Schedule I bank, convert at commercial discretion, and settle non-resident supplier invoices without central-bank approval.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (BoC) as the central monetary authority; Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) as the federal prudential supervisor of Schedule I and II banks; Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC) as the financial intelligence unit administering the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA), cross-border currency and monetary instruments reporting, and money services business (MSB) registration and supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium on a licensed-sponsored basis, and not viable as a standalone direct-settlement treasury rail. Canada has no statutory EMI category, and UK or EU EMI licences do not passport into British Columbia. An offshore digital EMI may lawfully act only as a technology and FX-liquidity wrapper, while all CAD-denominated clearing, settlement, merchant payouts, and payout reserves must route through a FINTRAC-registered Canadian MSB or a Schedule I bank sponsor, such as the Stripe Canada Ltd. MSB arrangement and Escrow_Pay's Canadian sponsor-bank structure. Direct treasury domiciliation solely in an offshore EMI settlement account is non-compliant for CAD inflows; the ULC must maintain a registered Canadian bank account as its treasury master account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound funding for the BC ULC is effected by equity subscription or by non-resident shareholder loan. Compliant routing requires a SWIFT MT103 wire with a standard purpose-of-payment narrative and ISO 20022 external purpose code EQUI for equity injection or INTC for intercompany loan; USD is cleared over Fedwire or CHIPS, CAD over Lynx; the beneficiary account is held in the ULC name at a BC branch of a Schedule I bank. Bank-side verification requires a share subscription agreement, board resolution, share certificates, an executed shareholder loan agreement with arm's-length interest, BC corporate register extracts, and a CPA flow-through-funds letter validating source of wealth. Investment Canada Act notification to Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada is triggered only where enterprise value exceeds the applicable threshold, currently C$5M or higher for WTO investors.Disbursements for ISIC 4791 luxury resale are cleared through five rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 bank wires for consignor payouts and high-value auction procurements, with OFAC/UN/EU sanctions pre-screening and FINTRAC EFT reporting when the wire is at or above C$10,000; (2) documentary commercial letters of credit, sight or usance, for EU and Asian wholesale suppliers; (3) Canadian domestic clearing through Lynx and Real-Time Rail for CAD obligations; (4) Stripe Connect and Escrow_Pay API payout rails for marketplace disbursements to consignors and settlements; (5) multi-currency batch payouts through a bank-sponsored treasury platform for non-CAD beneficiaries.
