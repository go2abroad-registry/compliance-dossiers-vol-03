Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Companies and Personal Property Security Branch, Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery and Procurement (ServiceOntario); federal interface via Canada Revenue Agency Business Registration Online and Environment and Climate Change Canada for export controls.

Governing statute

No federal limited partnership statute exists in Canada; formation is effected under a provincial Limited Partnerships Act (e.g., Ontario Limited Partnerships Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. L.8, ss. 2, 8-9) and the Partnership Act; federal-scope compliance is achieved through Canada Revenue Agency Business Number registration, extra-provincial LP declarations, the Income Tax Act, R.S.C. 1985, c. 1 (5th Supp.), s. 96 for pass-through taxation, and the Scrap Metal Dealers Act, 2009, S.O. 2009, c. 5 for industry licensing.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form 1 - Declaration of Limited Partnership, together with the Limited Partnership Agreement (retained document) and, where inter-provincial operations commence, the Extra-Provincial Limited Partnership Declaration; followed by CRA Business Number registration (Form RC1).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 55 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow account requirement for limited partnerships in Canada; capital is fixed by the Limited Partnership Agreement, may be contributed in cash, in kind, or by binding promise, and the initial partner contributions remain subject to the receiving financial institution's source-of-funds verification and Canada's Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act client identification duties.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification and annual inspection under the Ontario Fire Code (O. Reg. 213/07), including hot work permits and approved extinguishing systems; Electrical Safety Authority inspection certificate for the shredding, granulating, and weighing equipment; structural load inspection certificate for the processing platform and overhead storage racks; Occupational Health and Safety Act compliance (guardrails, lockout/tagout, machine guarding, personal protective equipment, and WHMIS-GHS program); and Workplace Safety and Insurance Board registration and clearance letter confirming no outstanding premiums before operation commences.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow account requirement for limited partnerships in Canada; capital is fixed by the Limited Partnership Agreement, may be contributed in cash, in kind, or by binding promise, and the initial partner contributions remain subject to the receiving financial institution's source-of-funds verification and Canada's Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act client identification duties.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) / Agence du revenu du Canada; portal: CRA My Business Account – https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency.html

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity carrying on commercial activity in Canada must register for a federal Business Number (BN) and GST/HST account using Form RC1 or via CRA's online registration in My Business Account. Proof of foreign registration, authorized director identification, and Canadian business address may be required. The BN is issued within approximately 2-4 weeks; the GST/HST account is activated simultaneously or upon separate application.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign entity carrying on commercial activity in Canada must register for a federal Business Number (BN) and GST/HST account using Form RC1 or via CRA's online registration in My Business Account. Proof of foreign registration, authorized director identification, and Canadian business address may be required. The BN is issued within approximately 2-4 weeks; the GST/HST account is activated simultaneously or upon separate application.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Canada does not operate a check-the-box regime. Limited partnerships are statutorily fiscally transparent for federal income tax and GST/HST purposes. Available elections include the partnership fiscal-period election (Income Tax Act s. 249.1) and the GST/HST election to have a fiscal year different from the calendar year. Hybrid mismatch final rules under the Income Tax Act also apply to the extent of deduction timing mismatches, but no elective classification is offered.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 in worldwide annual taxable supplies (the 'small supplier' threshold) for persons not registered; non-residents without a Canadian permanent establishment may also trigger registration if they make taxable supplies to recipients in Canada that are not GST/HST registered.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to supplies of goods exported from Canada, including recycled scrap copper wire, when the supplier obtains proof of export, title passes to a non-resident recipient, and the goods are delivered outside Canada. Offshore services directly to non-residents may also qualify if not for use in Canada. All conditions under Schedule VI, Part V, sections 1 and 5 must be met.

  • Canada Customs Export Declaration (B13A or EDI/EEI equivalent)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing export to the named foreign consignee
  • Commercial invoice with an explicit statement of export and destination country
  • Proof of export such as customs export stamps, port authority records, or forwarding agent confirmation
  • Inward remittance certificate or SWIFT/LC evidence showing payment from the non-resident buyer

No federal real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate exists. CRA accepts electronic invoices and records, and e-invoicing via EDI or XML is voluntary. There is no API-based continuous transaction control in Canada.

CRA can reassess zero-rated exports on audit where documentary proof is incomplete or inconsistent, thereby clawing back input tax credits and imposing net GST/HST owed, interest from the tax liability date, and penalties of up to 6% plus a second-tier penalty for repeated omissions. Fraudulent claims may attract income tax fraud penalties up to 50% of the estimated tax avoided and criminal or civil prosecution.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada operates a floating exchange rate regime with unrestricted convertibility for current account transactions including trade payments and profit remittances. Capital account transactions are permitted with mandatory FINTRAC reporting for values exceeding CAD 10,000. Profit remittances from Canadian entities to foreign partners are allowed without restriction provided tax compliance and beneficial ownership disclosure are maintained.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement exists for surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds. Receipts may be received and deposited directly in foreign currency. All exports valued at CAD 2,000 or more must be reported to the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA). No conversion ratios or surrender timelines apply.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada
