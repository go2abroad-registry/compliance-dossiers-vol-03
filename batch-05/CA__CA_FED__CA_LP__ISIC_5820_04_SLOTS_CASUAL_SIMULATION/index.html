Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) / Agence du revenu du Canada (ARC) - national tax authority; online portals: My Business Account (MyBA) and Business Registration Online (BRO) at canada.ca/en/revenue-agency

E-government filing portal

The CA LP is fiscally transparent for federal income tax purposes but constitutes a separate person registrable for GST/HST. To procure its statutory identifier, the LP, acting through its general partner or authorized Canadian agent, files Form RC1 (Request for a Business Number and Registration) or registers through CRA Business Registration Online (BRO). CRA issues a 15-digit Business Number (BN) plus an RT (GST/HST) program account. Non-resident partners must disclose their foreign TINs for FATCA/CRS reporting and Part XIII withholding matters. Where the LP is non-resident or controlled by non-residents, CRA may require GST/HST security under Excise Tax Act (Canada), s.241, and the LP must appoint a representative for service of documents and records access.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the LP is fiscally transparent for Canadian federal income tax (income computed under Income Tax Act (Canada), s.96 and allocated to partners; no entity-level tax), but it is a separate GST/HST registrant for taxable commercial activity. Non-resident partners of a Canadian LP carrying on business in Canada remain subject to Part I tax on their allocated Canadian-source income, subject to applicable treaty provisions.

Available election forms

No check-the-box regime exists under Canadian federal law; an LP is statutorily classified as a partnership. Available elections: (i) partnership fiscal-period election under ITA s.249.1; (ii) GST/HST Quick Method election under ETA s.227 (where eligibility criteria are met); (iii) annual GST/HST filing election under ETA s.238; (iv) designation of a partnership representative for GST/HST administration; (v) elections under ITA ss.216/217 available to non-resident partners with Canadian-source passive income, which do not extend to active social-casino publishing income; (vi) hybrid-mismatch deduction rules under ITA ss.18.2/18.3 operate as mandatory anti-avoidance limitations without an elective opt-out, though transparent-allocation alignments may be made in partnership filings.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.05%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 in worldwide taxable supplies over the trailing four calendar quarters (small supplier threshold, ETA s.123(1)); new registrants are tested on a per-quarter basis at CAD 10,000. All app-based social-casino revenues via Apple IAP and Google Play Billing must be aggregated with other taxable supplies for threshold determination.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of simulated-casino software, virtual slots content, and in-app digital tokens to non-residents of Canada qualify for 0% GST/HST under ETA Schedule VI Part V ss.7 and 10 where the recipient is a non-resident not registered under ETA and the supply is not in respect of real property or tangible personal property situated in Canada. The LP must substantiate non-resident recipient status, location of consumption, and the absence of Canadian-use nexus. Platform-mediated sales through Apple/Google require a true-supplier analysis: supplies characterized as made by the LP to the non-resident platform entity may be zero-rated, while supplies to Canadian consumers through the Canadian app-store channel remain taxable at the applicable GST/HST rate unless the marketplace operator is the deemed supplier.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Form B13A) - applicable to physical goods only; inapplicable to digital intangible exports
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill - applicable to physical goods only; inapplicable to digital intangible exports

Canada does not operate a mandatory real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime for e-invoicing. The CRA voluntarily supports a Pan-Canadian e-invoicing framework, but the LP remains obligated to file GST/HST returns electronically through GST/HST NETFILE or CRA-approved software, to preserve machine-readable books and records for at least six years, and to retain Apple IAP / Google Play billing statements, settlement reports, and commercial invoices. Invoices must disclose the BN, supplier identity, date, taxable amount, GST/HST charged, and explicit zero-rated designation for export supplies.

CRA audits of zero-rated digital exports center on transfer pricing, marketplace recharacterization, and true-supplier attribution. Erroneous or fraudulent zero-rating claims expose the LP to: (i) reassessment of net tax with clawback of associated input tax credits; (ii) extension of the normal four-year reassessment window where misrepresentation is established; (iii) penalties under ETA s.280 (6% of the tax amount, increased upon subsequent infractions) and false-statement penalties under ETA s.280.1; (iv) criminal prosecution under ETA s.327 and Criminal Code s.380 for intentional evasion; and (v) daily compounded interest at prescribed CRA rates. Canada imposes no statutory 180-day FX repatriation deadline; however, the 180-day benchmark is retained as an administrative documentation standard for linking export consideration to zero-rated sales, and unrepatriated or delayed receipts will attract heightened evidentiary scrutiny.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Canadian Dollar (CAD) is freely floating and fully convertible under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. Canada maintains an open capital account with no exchange-control restrictions on current or capital transactions, including inbound capital contributions, outward profit repatriation, and foreign-currency borrowing by a Canadian Limited Partnership. Profit remittances to non-resident partners face no convertibility or permit hurdle; the fiscal overlay consists of Part I income tax on the non-resident partner's share of Canadian-source business income (reported via T5013) and, for distributions sourced from designated payments such as royalties or interest, Part XIII withholding at a 25% statutory rate typically reduced under an applicable tax treaty.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Canada imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no repatriation timeline, and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Receipts from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing may be retained in CAD or USD indefinitely at treasury discretion. For Canadian income tax reporting, all foreign-currency revenue must be converted into CAD at the Bank of Canada's daily published exchange rate under the Income Tax Act.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada) as the central bank; Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) as the federal banking regulator; Department of Finance Canada (Minister of Finance) as the residual authority. Canada has no dedicated foreign-exchange administration agency; exchange controls were abolished in 1951.
