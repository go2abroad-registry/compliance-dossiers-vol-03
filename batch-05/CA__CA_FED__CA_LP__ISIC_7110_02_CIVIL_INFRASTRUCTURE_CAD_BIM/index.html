Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) / Agence du revenu du Canada (ARC); digital portals: CRA 'My Business Account' and Business Registration Online (BRO) at canada.ca.

E-government filing portal

The non-resident CA_LP must obtain a 9-digit Business Number (BN) from the CRA. Steps: (1) confirm Canadian tax nexus (carrying on business in Canada under ITA s.253 or making taxable supplies in Canada); (2) register via BRO or Form RC1 'Request for a Business Number', electing the RT (GST/HST) program account and, where applicable, the RP (partnership income tax information return) program; (3) the partnership is a separate person for GST/HST purposes (ETA s.272.1) and files GST/HST returns under its own BN; (4) file Form T5013 Partnership Information Return with Schedule 50 to allocate income and losses to non-resident partners; (5) maintain a Canadian representative for service of process where the LP is not resident but carries on business in Canada; no minimum capital is required for tax registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — The non-resident CA_LP must obtain a 9-digit Business Number (BN) from the CRA. Steps: (1) confirm Canadian tax nexus (carrying on business in Canada under ITA s.253 or making taxable supplies in Canada); (2) register via BRO or Form RC1 'Request for a Business Number', electing the RT (GST/HST) program account and, where applicable, the RP (partnership income tax information return) program; (3) the partnership is a separate person for GST/HST purposes (ETA s.272.1) and files GST/HST returns under its own BN; (4) file Form T5013 Partnership Information Return with Schedule 50 to allocate income and losses to non-resident partners; (5) maintain a Canadian representative for service of process where the LP is not resident but carries on business in Canada; no minimum capital is required for tax registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Canada has no IRC §7701-style check-the-box regime. Available elections include: (a) ITA s.96(3) election to deem the partnership a separate person for designated provisions; (b) hybrid mismatch integrity rules (ITA ss.18.2, 18.21, 20.2, effective July 1, 2022) which disallow deduction outcomes from cross-border transparent/non-transparent mismatches and cannot be overridden by taxpayer election; (c) ETA s.156 election for GST/HST grouping of closely related persons, and ETA s.273 election to treat certain supplies between a partner and the partnership as made for nil consideration; (d) ITA s.216 election permitting a non-resident to file a net-basis income tax return for Canadian rental or business income.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 (small supplier threshold under ETA s.148(1)); a non-resident LP whose worldwide taxable supplies sourced to Canada are at or below CAD 30,000 in the preceding four calendar quarters and in the current quarter is not required to register, but may voluntarily register to claim input tax credits; zero-rated export supplies are ignored for the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Offshore BIM/CAD drafting services for a foreign civil-infrastructure project qualify for 0% GST/HST under ETA Schedule VI, Part V, ss.7 and 10: the supply is made to a non-resident person and is not in respect of real property or tangible personal property situated in Canada, nor rendered to an individual present in Canada. Electronically delivered CAD/BIM files are intangible personal property supplied to the non-resident for use outside Canada. The supplier must demonstrate the foreign situs of the underlying project and the non-resident status of the recipient.

  • Executed export service agreement identifying the non-resident recipient and the non-Canadian project situs
  • Itemized export invoice denominated in foreign currency showing '0% GST/HST' and the partnership's BN
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 evidencing foreign-currency payment repatriation within 180 days
  • Non-resident status evidence (Certificate of Residence or IRS Form W-8BEN-E)
  • T5013 Schedule 50 income allocation schedule for partner-level filing

Canada does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, API-based government clearance, or digital stamping for invoices. The CRA has adopted a voluntary Peppol PINT-based B2B e-invoicing interoperability framework. GST/HST registrants with annual taxable revenues exceeding CAD 1.5 million must file returns electronically via GST/HST NETFILE; all registrants must retain original invoices, contracts, and delivery evidence for six years under ETA s.286.

CRA may reassess wrongfully zero-rated supplies to 5% GST plus daily compound interest (ETA ss.280, 281). Gross-negligence penalty under ETA s.280(1) is the greater of CAD 250 and 25% of the net tax amount, and is recoverable from the partnership. Failure-to-file penalty under ETA s.285 is 1% per month up to 12 months plus 6% annual interest. Tax evasion under ETA s.327.5 may attract fines up to 200% of the tax evaded and/or imprisonment up to 5 years. Input tax credits claimed on related expenditures are retrospectively clawed back if the zero-rating is reversed; the standard CRA reassessment window is 4 years (ETA s.298), extended indefinitely in cases of misrepresentation or fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada maintains IMF Article VIII status and allows unrestricted payments and transfers for current international transactions. The capital account is broadly open; inbound investment is screened mainly through the Investment Canada Act, and profit remittances from a Canadian limited partnership are not subject to exchange-control approval. Remittance hurdles are tax-related rather than FX-related: Part XIII withholding on distributions to non-residents (generally a 25% statutory rate, often reduced by treaty) and GST/HST registration responsibilities must be resolved. CAD is fully convertible for residents and non-residents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Canadian law does not impose a surrender requirement, mandatory conversion ratio, or statutory repatriation timeline for export receipts. Export invoices may be paid in convertible foreign currency and held offshore or converted voluntarily; only the CAD equivalent must be reported for income tax and GST/HST purposes. EFT inflows of CAD 10,000 or more trigger FINTRAC reporting obligations for the receiving financial institution, without restricting convertibility.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada); federal prudential bank supervision is delegated to the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI); AML/CFT oversight is delegated to the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC). There is no separate foreign-exchange administration because Canada has abandoned exchange controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low as the primary banking venue; Medium as a supplementary disbursement rail. A foreign EMI or cross-border payments fintech such as Wise is not licensed to accept deposit liabilities in Canada and must register as a money services business with FINTRAC if it provides transfer services to Canadian counterparties. It cannot serve as the capital escrow, formal liquidity account, or regulatory reporting hub for the LP. Use it only for low-value vendor payments after maintaining an approved vendor and payment ceiling, with a subsidiary CAD bank account for legality.

Route through SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 COV where a chain bank is required) to the beneficiary account held at a Canadian Schedule I bank. The payment narrative must identify the Canadian LP beneficiary, the account number, and the purpose such as LP capital contribution; if the sending bank requires an ISO 20022 purpose code, use a capital or investment transfer category that matches the executed capital call.
