Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A registered office in BC must be a physical street address in the province, not a blind mailbox. Virtual office services are accepted as operational support (mail forwarding, meeting rooms, telephony), but statutory registered office must be a real BC address available for service of process and record inspection. For aircraft-engine dry leasing, no local leasing premises are statutorily required; nevertheless, CRA and tax authorities will expect actual management, books and records, board activities, and economic wherewithal beyond a virtual mailing address.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada maintains full current and capital account convertibility, observing IMF Article VIII obligations. There are no general exchange controls, no capital import licensing, no repatriation ceilings, and no restrictions on payment of lease rentals, dividends, interest, or equity redemptions. Cross-border transfers must still pass bank AML/KYC, sanctions, and CRS checks; Canadian withholding tax under Income Tax Act Part XIII applies to payments to non-residents. ULC distributions and interest on shareholder loans may carry withholding rates reduced by applicable tax treaties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory foreign-exchange surrender or mandatory conversion rules exist in Canada. Exporters may retain and disburse foreign currency freely. Canadian financial institutions must report large cross-border transactions of C$10,000 or equivalent under PCMLTFA reporting rules, and all payments must be screened against Canadian economic sanctions, especially under the Special Economic Measures Act and the Criminal Code. Proceeds from aircraft engine leasing may be settled in USD or other currencies without conversion obligation.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada); no separate foreign-exchange-control administration exists. Exchange control policy is shared with Finance Canada, the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions, and the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium as a supplementary payment rail; Low as the primary treasury bank. Canada does not legally recognize the UK/EU-style EMI. Fintech payment companies such as Wise Canada and Payoneer operate as money services businesses or retail payment service providers subject to the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act, the Retail Payment Activities Act, and provincial money-services laws. They cannot take deposits from the public, cannot provide settlement guarantees, and are not clearing members; therefore they are unsuitable as sole repositories for capital escrow or large aircraft-engine leasing liquidity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Canadian banks route inbound equity and shareholder-loan injections primarily through SWIFT MT103 or MT202, using Lynx for high-value CAD settlement and standard CHIPS/Fedwire equivalents for USD. Canada does not mandate central-bank purpose codes; wire narrative fields should state 'ULC capital contribution', 'Shareholder loan to Canadian treasury', or 'Equity injection for aircraft engine leasing operations' to ensure compliance. Financial institutions require board resolutions, executed loan or subscription agreements, CRS and FATCA self-certifications, source-of-funds evidence, and beneficial-ownership particulars. Funds may initially be held in a capital escrow account pending KYC completion and then released to the operating account.Outbound lease rental payments, maintenance reserves, security-deposit returns, and vendor disbursements clear via SWIFT MT103 MT103 for USD and other cross-border currencies, with high-value CAD cleared through Lynx and intra-Canada transfers through the Automated Funds Transfer System as applicable. Commercial letters of credit issued by Canadian banks are appropriate for structured engine lease security deposits and delivery-condition milestones.
