Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) / Agence du revenu du Canada (ARC); portal: My Business Account (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/e-services/digital-services-businesses/business-account.html)

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities incorporating a federal/provincial business corporation must obtain a 9-digit Business Number (BN) from CRA by submitting Form RC1 (Request for a Business Number) upon incorporation via Corporations Canada or provincial registry; BN is required before opening payroll/RP account, GST/HST/RT account, and corporate income tax (RC) account. Non-resident shareholders must provide NR4/T2 filings; registration can be completed online via Business Registration Online (BRO) or mailed to the nearest CRA tax centre.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign entities incorporating a federal/provincial business corporation must obtain a 9-digit Business Number (BN) from CRA by submitting Form RC1 (Request for a Business Number) upon incorporation via Corporations Canada or provincial registry; BN is required before opening payroll/RP account, GST/HST/RT account, and corporate income tax (RC) account. Non-resident shareholders must provide NR4/T2 filings; registration can be completed online via Business Registration Online (BRO) or mailed to the nearest CRA tax centre.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Available: (1) S. 44.1 ITA deemed taxation year-end election to align subsidiary fiscal year with foreign parent; (2) s. 150(2) ITA nil return election for types of corporations; (3) s. 151.1 ETA election to file GST/HST annually (non-registered small suppliers); (4) s. 156 ETA election for closely related corporations to treat supplies as nil-consideration; (5) S. 152(4) waiver for extended reassessment window. NOTE: Canada does not operate a US-style check-the-box entity classification; the Income Tax Act statutorily characterises the Business Corporation as a corporation (entity-level taxation). Hybrid mismatch rules under ss. 18.2/18.3 ITA apply to deny deduction for dividends on hybrid instruments.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CAD 30,000 (small supplier threshold: CAD 30,000 per single calendar quarter or CAD 30,000 or more in the last four consecutive calendar quarters; mandatory registration applies once exceeded except for non-resident zero-rated service suppliers)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating available under Part V of Schedule VI ETA: (1) supply of a service to a non-resident (s. 7) where the service does not relate to real property in Canada or tangible personal property situated in Canada at the time performed, and the non-resident is not registered for GST/HST - applicable to PCT patent prosecution and international trademark services supplied to non-resident applicants; (2) exports of tangible personal property zero-rated under s. 6 and s. 17 (satisfactory evidence of export within 60 days); (3) assignment/licence of IP (s. 7.1 and s. 11). Documentary evidence required: signed contract identifying non-resident status, client's foreign address, evidence of payment remittance, and corporate registry extract.

  • Commercial Invoice (detailed service/IP description and foreign party)
  • Customs Export Declaration (CBSA eExport Report / Form B13A for physical goods; for services, CRA Form GST11 plus non-resident certificate)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (for physical exhibits or patent priority documents)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (wire transfer proof, e.g., SWIFT MT103, Wise transaction receipt)
  • Signed Service Agreement (indicating non-resident recipient and place of supply)
  • Certificate of Non-Residency (foreign registry extract or passport copy)

No statutory real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API clearance requirement (unlike Brazil/Italy). Voluntary PEPPOL-based E-invoicing (Canada Pan-Canadian Trust Framework) applies for B2G procurement under the federal e-invoicing initiative effective 2024-2025. CBSA CARM (CBSA Assessment and Revenue Management) handles import/export accounting via portal (eManifest/Export Reporting). GST/HST returns are filed electronically via CRA NETFILE-approved software; Retail Payment Activities Act API reporting applies only to PSPs.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada maintains free convertibility for current account transactions under the Payments and Systems Act. Capital account transactions require Bank of Canada approval for certain investments exceeding thresholds, but profit remittances are generally unrestricted subject to tax compliance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds required in Canada. All foreign exchange receipts must be reported to the Bank of Canada via the Proceeds of Dispositions Reporting System if applicable. For service-based income such as patent prosecution fees, remittances are processed freely without conversion ratios or surrender obligations.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada
